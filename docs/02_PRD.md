# Product requirements

## User outcome
From a phone, a BS23 seller can turn a target-company search into a qualified account with evidence, a transparent score, a sales angle, and an immediate next action.

## Phase-1 acceptance criteria
- ICP criteria persist in the browser and can be changed without code.
- A researched account stores company, country, research fields, and an evidence ledger.
- Result labels are qualified (65+), review (40–64), or not-a-fit (<40), with per-criterion explanation.
- A source-less claim is never required to be called FACT.
- Accounts can be exported as JSON.

## Non-goals
Automatic web scraping, autonomous outreach, CRM replacement, and meeting transcription are out of Phase 1.
